Urimai (உரிமை)

Voice first welfare scheme screening for Tamil speakers. A Tamil voice note goes in; the schemes a person likely qualifies for come out, with the office that confirms each one. The LLM extracts facts but never decides: a rules engine does, and "not yet asked" is never treated as "no".
